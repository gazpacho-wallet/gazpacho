            Not settings

            The app decides these alone. There is no switch for them, in Settings or under “Show advanced”.

            • Every safe-to-sign check. Your conditions can only be stricter.
            • Saving before sending, and never using a one-time signing secret twice.
            • Comparing at least two chain servers, and stopping when they disagree.
            • Answering an old state, and leaving before the factory ends.
            • Refusing mainnet in this release.
            • Signing once you are marked ready and the lobby starts. Readying is the consent; there is no question in the middle of a round.

                  Strengths

                  • The clearest separation of scopes. Each option has one tab, and a lobby's terms never share a page with an input that could look like it edits them.
                  • A newcomer sees few controls: profiles, seven limits and two choices about automation. Everything else waits behind one switch that is easy to find.
                  • The Checks card gives a first-time member a path and gives a returning member a ten-second health read, with the same four lines.
                  • Every commitment (join, automatic joining, pay, leave alone, and buying room in the 1.1.0 preview) uses the same summary panel, so the moment of consent is recognisable.
                  • One app and one home. Hosting is always one click away, but a person who never hosts sees only its calm starting page and nothing of it on Home.
                  • Terms show fixed rules, signed ranges and a rules fingerprint, and the fit check covers the whole range, so the summary panel stays true until the ready check.
                  • The same structure works on a phone and on a desktop without a second design.

                  Weaknesses

                  • Conditions are the core of the product and they sit in Settings. The summary on Lobbies and the Checks line carry them, but editing is always two taps away from the lobby that prompted it.
                  • The guided flows are long. Leaving alone has four steps before anything happens, and joining adds a summary on top of the fit check. An experienced member will find this slow.
                  • “Show advanced” is per page, so a member must discover it six times. An application-wide switch would be simpler to explain but would unfold everything at once.
                  • The Hosting area is thin compared with a console layout. A host with a ladder of thirty factories needs tables and bulk actions that this calm style does not suit.
                  • Ranges add a second number to two rows of every terms card and fit check. It is honest, but busier than a single value.
                  • Version 1.1.0 items are hidden in the normal view, so “can receive” has no next step beyond the one line saying more room comes later.
                  • The dashboard repeats facts that the status strip already shows (next date, watching, automatic joining).

                  What I would change after seeing it

                  • Promote My conditions out of Settings to its own side-bar entry, and keep the other five tabs as Settings.
                  • Let the fit check link each “My limit” cell to that one field, opened in place on the conditions page, so that a failing row is one tap from its fix.
                  • Shorten the join flow for a member who has joined this host before: fit check and summary on one page.
                  • Make the status strip change with the state (waiting to start, signing, leaving alone). In this mock-up it is fixed text.
                  • Give the Hosting area a denser “table” level, as a second use of the two-level idea.
                  • Test whether members understand that the Checks card can go back from done to not done, for example when a backup grows old.
