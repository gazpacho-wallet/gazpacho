        Every Gazpacho wallet can host. Hosting means opening a lobby, gathering people, and funding the factory they share. The more people host, the more factories there are for everyone. You have not hosted anything yet, so there is nothing to look after here.

        Hosting in one minute

        • You fund it. Each member's channel comes from your coins on chain, and they stay inside the factory for its whole life, about a month.
        • You keep the app running at the start, while members sign, and until your funding is sent; later also at each move to your next factory.
        • You earn fees: an optional joining fee, and a fee on each payment your members make.
        • What can cost you: chain fees for the funding; if members leave alone, part of the tree goes on chain at your cost; and your coins are tied up until the factory ends. Nobody can take them, but you cannot spend them early.
