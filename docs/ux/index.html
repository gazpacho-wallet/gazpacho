H, scored afterwards by the same critic: 38. Honesty rose from 2 to 4; quality fell from 4 to 3 because H is heavier than G (the home repeats items, Settings has two overlapping tables). Not ready as it stands: nine sentences are still stronger than the specifications, six findings against G are not fixed, and it predates the owner's answers to questions 3.

The spread is small: 30 to 37. A, B and F tie at 35 and differ mostly in their outer shell, so choosing between them is taste. G leads because it is the only concept where pause and the test-network marker never scroll away, and because of its live "why nothing fits" line and a "what happens at zero" sentence on every date.

The finding that matters most: every concept over-promised on safety

All seven scored 2 of 5 on honesty, for the same reasons. These are fixed in H and written into the brief as rules for any future screen:

  • "The app always leaves alone in time." True only while the app is running and has funds for fees.
  • "Nothing was lost" after being away too long. The app cannot know that; it can only say what it checked.
  • "Nothing moved, you are back in line" after a signing round that did not finish. Once a member's signatures have gone out, both the old and the new state must be treated as possible. No concept had a state for this.
  • "The steps wait for you." Timed steps have deadlines that pass whether the app is open or not.
  • "Your money is safe." Replaced by what the protection is and what it needs.

My own earlier word search missed these; the critic found them by reading for meaning.

Questions for you that the concepts expose

  • "Fits only now": a lobby whose current value fits but whose signed range does not. Grouped with the lobbies that do not fit. Is that the right wording?
  • "Profile" meant two things. H calls a member's starting sets "presets" and keeps "profile" for the host's lobby type.
  • Hosting funds: one wallet with a "set aside for hosting" amount, or a separate wallet? Leaning: one wallet.
  • Hosting on phones takes a tab slot from members who never host. Accept it, or put hosting in the header on phones only?
  • Do members need some on-chain coins to leave alone in a fee spike? The app can recommend a reserve; it cannot require one without breaking "members start at zero".
  • Which limit a refusal names: recommendation: whichever rule failed, the member's own limit or the host's signed promise, said plainly.
