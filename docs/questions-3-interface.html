Gazpacho questions 3: the interface

2026-11-11. Four questions the layout concepts raised. Each has options, an explanation and my recommendation. 1"Fits only now": the label for a lobby that fits today but could stop fitting

A lobby's adjustable terms have a signed range, and the host may move the value inside it until the ready check. By your earlier decision, the app compares your limit with the whole range, so you can never be switched at the last second.

That leaves one awkward case. Your limit on the joining fee is 2,000 sats. The lobby charges 1,800 now, but may move anywhere up to 2,500. Today's value fits; the range does not.

Paprika Post Joining fee 1,800 now (may move 1,000 to 2,500) Your limit 2,000
Concept D called this "Fits only now" and listed it with the lobbies that do not fit. If you have chosen the looser condition ("ready me while the current value fits"), the same lobby counts as fitting.
Recommendation: A. It keeps the safe default visible and explains itself in one tap. The research rule we adopted is "never hide a lobby that does not fit; grey it with the reason".

2Hosting funds: one wallet or two?

Everyone can host. A host needs a large amount on chain: in the sample, 22.5 to 37.5 million sats for one lobby. The question is whether that sits in the same wallet as the person's own coins.

Hosting also needs a linked Lightning node, which is separate whatever you choose here. This question is only about the on-chain coins that fund factories.
Recommendation: A for 1.0.0, with B as a later option for serious hosts. Most people who host will start small and occasionally. One wallet with a ceiling is the least to get wrong. B is cheap to add later because the keys already follow the standard account structure.

3Hosting on phones: a tab, or somewhere quieter?

You decided everyone can always host, with no role question. On a computer that costs nothing: the side menu has room. On a phone, the bottom bar holds four or five tabs, and a Hosting tab takes one from members who never host.

Phone, option A: Home · Pay · Lobbies · Activity · Host Phone, option B: Home · Pay · Lobbies · Activity (Host in the top-right menu) Phone, option C: Home · Pay · Lobbies · Activity (Host appears as a tab once you host)
Concepts A and D gave Hosting a tab; concept G put it in the header on phones. The critique noted a daily host may overlook a header link.
Recommendation: C. It keeps "everyone can always host" without a role question, and puts the tab where the work is. The one cost is that the phone layout changes once, on the first hosted lobby; the app says so in one line when it happens.

4A small on-chain reserve for members

Members start with nothing and need nothing to join. But leaving alone means putting transactions on chain. Those carry fees fixed when the factory was built, plus an anchor that lets someone add more fee later. In a fee spike, the fixed fees may be too low, and only a member with some on-chain coins can add more.

The channel research also found a related gap: the standard taproot channel's follow-up transactions for payments in flight carry no fee of their own, so a member with no coins cannot claim them in a spike. Both point the same way. In 1.1.0, a reserve is also how a member with a shared leaf leaves at more than its coverage.
Recommendation: C. B for 1.0.0 keeps the door open to people with nothing while being honest about fee spikes. In 1.1.0 the requirement applies only where the money is genuinely above what the payout protects, which is exactly where the design itself says a member needs on-chain coins.
