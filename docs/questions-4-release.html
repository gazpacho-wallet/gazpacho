Gazpacho questions 4: shaping 1.0.0

2026-11-12. Two decisions that don't affect the first signet slice but shape the first release. Each has the background, options, and my recommendation. Pick one per question, add notes, then press "Copy my answers" and paste them back. Nothing is sent anywhere.

1Timed levels in the first release

Background

A SuperScalar tree has two kinds of levels:

Fixed levelTimed level (Decker-Wattenhofer)
What it allowsNothing changes under it without a new factoryThe members under it can re-sign their part of the tree (a "layer update") without everyone in the factory
Cost to leave aloneAbout nothing extraAbout 3 days extra per timed level (432 blocks at the planned settings)
Risk while awayNone from the treeOnce a layer has been updated, an old version could be published, and must be answered within about one day, or the member loses the difference

The finding that changes things: in 1.0.0, nothing ever updates a timed layer. With one member per leaf, no liquidity sales (those are 1.1.0), and payments that never advance the tree, members move to the next factory instead of re-signing layers. So in 1.0.0 timed levels bring only the longer exit, and the one-day risk never actually arises. This rests on reading the code; it is recorded as open point O1 and will be confirmed with a simulator test and against the design posts.

This revisits an earlier decision recorded in the knowledge base: "Full layered tree in the first release; build the fixed tree first." Option A below changes it.

What sets how long a member can safely be away, then? Without timed levels, the remaining risk is the ordinary Lightning one: the host publishing an old channel state, which the member must answer within the channel's revocation delay. That delay is a channel setting the terms can choose, as in ordinary Lightning (often one to two weeks). The exact value belongs to the channel specification, still to be written; treat "about two weeks" as the target, not yet a promise.

Recommendation: A. It gives 1.0.0 members the shortest exits and the simplest safety story, with nothing lost: what timed levels are for arrives in 1.1.0 together with them. It also resolves open point O2 (the on-chain reserve): without timed-layer races, members need no fee coins to defend the tree.

2Do seats in a lobby cost anything?

Background

Today a member joins a lobby for free and puts in nothing. That is the point of Gazpacho: people with no bitcoin can get a Lightning channel. But free seats cause three problems:

  1. Jamming a lobby. One person with many fresh keys can take seats and never sign, or use up a lobby's seat limit in about half a minute, so nobody else can join. Money is never at risk, but lobbies fail.
  2. Stalling the host. Once the end-date payout exists (before mainnet), a member with a zero balance is still paid its full channel size if the host fails to close properly. Free seats make it cheap to join in numbers and go silent, hoping the host misses a deadline.
  3. Absent members cost the host. Each member who never shows up at wind-down costs the host an exit on chain. With many free, absent members, hosting gets expensive.

The difficulty: a member starts with nothing, so it cannot pay a deposit out of its own channel. Any money-based seat cost needs coins from outside, which shuts out the people Gazpacho most wants to reach.

Recommendation: B for 1.0.0, growing into D later. A proof costs the honest member nothing (the coin is never spent, never even moved), makes fake seats cost real bitcoin, and keeps newcomer seats open for people with nothing. It reuses the proof checks discovery already needs. Deposits (C) can be added later as one more choice in the terms, once a trustless way to hold them is designed.
