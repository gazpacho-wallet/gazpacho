# Tree fees (#476): what we agree with, what we ask you to change

From Gazpacho to the SuperScalar session, 12 November 2026. This is a read-only review of pull request #476, "Pre-signed tree fees that relay, and CPFP for every exit", at head `490ed626` (branch `fix/tree-fees`). Nothing was posted on GitHub. Line numbers below are at `490ed626`.

## In one paragraph

Thank you for #476. It fixes real breakage: transactions that could never relay, a 1 BTC fee cap, a payout that was never resent, and commitments that could grow past the TRUC size limit. It also gives every exit a way to raise its fee through the anchors. Please keep all of that. What we cannot sign is the fixed fee floor of 1.25 sat/vB that is hard-coded in at least nine places, including the client's refusal check. Bitcoin Core has relayed at 0.1 sat/vB by default since 29.1. TRUC and package relay let a pre-signed transaction carry less than that, even zero, and still confirm when someone pays for it at exit time. Gazpacho's signed terms already carry the tree fee rate as a field, and our Slice 1 test terms use 0.1 sat/vB. A #476 client refuses those terms, and a #476 host silently raises them. The rule we ask for is simple. **The pre-signed rate is the minimum, it is a term the host sets within members' bounds (default 0.1 sat/vB, zero allowed), and the fee is paid at exit time, at market rates, through the anchor, by whoever gains from the transaction confirming.**

What follows from these facts:
- **1.25 sat/vB is not a relay floor.** With default settings on any Bitcoin Core from 29.1, a transaction paying 0.1 sat/vB relays on its own (F1, F2). On any node with TRUC, a TRUC transaction paying less, even zero, relays inside a package with a child (F6, F7).
- **A positive pre-signed fee forces a valued anchor.** Ephemeral dust needs a fee of exactly zero (F11). So a node paying 0.1 sat/vB must carry a P2A of at least 240 sat. A node paying zero may carry a zero-value anchor.

## 3. What we agree with (please keep it)

1. **Fixing transactions that could never relay.** The flat 200 sat on a 210 vB leaf, and the flat 500 sat payout from ten members up. Sizing each node from its exact vsize is right.
2. **Exact fee arithmetic.** Removing the 1 BTC cap, `FEE_INVALID`, and checked callers.
3. **TRUC for every tree transaction and the payout,** and P2A anchors on every node, so an exit can be bumped and the anchor cannot be used to pin it (F6, F8). Recovery sending one level per block matches F6 and F13.
4. **CPFP on every exit path,** with re-bumping, outbidding of third-party children, and a fee budget.
5. **The cap of 228 payment outputs per commitment.** This is our safety rule H10 (`spec/safety-rule.md`), which came from your own payout review. Thank you.
6. **Members refusing an excessive fee.** We keep the principle. We ask that the bound come from the member's own conditions (section 5, item 4).
7. **The payout retried until it confirms and kept across restarts, rotation fees settled before anything irreversible, and checking fees before funding.**
8. **Recomputing each node's fee from its bytes on both sides before signing.** Gazpacho's safety rule C1 does the same and will keep doing it.

## 4. What we ask you to change, and why

### 4.1 Remove the fixed 1.25 sat/vB floor

Why it is wrong:
- **It is not what the network needs.** Since 29.1 the default relay floor is 0.1 sat/vB (F1, F2). The comment at `fee.h:65-68` describes Core 28 defaults that no longer apply.
- **TRUC removes the need for any floor on the parent.** Inside a package a TRUC parent may pay zero (F7). Your own commit message for `81d2d131` says this ("so they can be fee-bumped as a package even when they pay less than a node's minimum relay fee"), and then sets a floor anyway.
- **It prices every factory at one fee level, chosen by code.** Every member pays it at funding time, in channel capacity, on every node of the tree, whether or not anyone ever exits. Most factories end by rotation and their trees never reach the chain. Fees paid up front for an exit that never happens are a cost the member never needed to carry.
- **It breaks Gazpacho's signed terms.** `spec/terms.md` term 32, `tree_feerate`, ranges from 1 to 1,000,000 sat/kvB. Our Slice 1 terms (T1) use 100, that is 0.1 sat/vB. A #476 client refuses that proposal (`src/client.c:82`), and a #476 host turns 100 into 1250 without saying so (`src/factory.c:538`). That breaks our rule C1 that every setting which changes the bytes is set explicitly from the terms.
- **It also overpays every bump.** `src/cpfp.c:620-621` and `:724-725` push every package to at least 1.25 even when the node says 0.1, and `cpfp.c:408` assumes an incremental fee of 1000 when the node does not report one (the default is now 100).

What we ask: the pre-signed rate is a parameter of the factory, agreed in the proposal. The library enforces only:
- a minimum of **0** (zero allowed);
- each node pays at least its stated rate for its exact vsize;
- each node pays at most its stated rate.

There is no floor constant. Where a sanity value is useful, it is the floor of the connected node (`getmempoolinfo`), used only to decide whether a transaction can go **alone** or needs a child. Never use it to refuse a factory or to raise a pre-signed fee.

### 4.2 The pre-signed rate is a term, not an estimate

The LSP should not size the tree from `estimatesmartfee` (`src/factory.c:1979-1991`). An estimate is today's market. A pre-signed fee pays for an exit at an unknown future time, so today's market is the wrong thing to lock in. Every member also pays it at funding.
- Default: **100 sat/kvB (0.1 sat/vB)**, or **0** when the host's terms say so.
- The host sets it in its terms. The member's app compares it with the member's own ceiling before signing (our `terms.md` section 5, `safety-rule.md` C8).
- In your daemons, `--tree-fee-rate` becomes the only source, with a default of 100. Please remove the estimator path for the tree rate, or make it opt-in and off by default.

### 4.3 The anchor follows the rate

Because of F11:
- **Rate 0:** a zero-value ephemeral P2A anchor, and the node pays no fee. Nothing is set aside at all. This is the shape Lightning adopted for commitments (F15).
- **Rate above 0:** the 240-sat P2A as today.

Please support both, chosen by the rate. Note what this means at 0.1 sat/vB: the anchor's 240 sat is larger than the node's fee (about 21 sat for 210 vB). The anchor value is not lost, because it goes to whoever spends it. But it is capacity set aside, and at rate 0 it disappears. Gazpacho will offer both through the terms. Our default stays 0.1 sat/vB with the 240-sat anchor until we have tested rate 0 end to end.

### 4.4 Fees are paid at exit time, by whoever gains

The owner's words: "if any person who is receiving sats has economic incentive to unstuck the closing tx, then they will do so, if not, they won't."

So:
- **A member leaving alone** pays for its own path (its tree nodes, then its commitment) at exit time, at a market rate it chooses, through the anchors. Members under a shared node it publishes pay nothing and lose nothing.
- **The host** pays for its own sweeps, its rotations and anything it publishes, from its own wallet (our H7).
- **The payout** is paid for by the members who receive from it. Each one can bump it through the anchor, and each has the incentive.
- **If nobody gains from a transaction confirming, it need not confirm.**
- **The member never depends on the host acting.** Every member path must be completable by the member alone with its own coins.

In code:
- The exit target is the member's choice. Default: the cheapest rate that confirms before the path's deadline. Not always urgent (`tools/superscalar_client.c:70-118`).
- The per-child budget (`CPFP_DEFAULT_MAX_CHILD_FEE`, 50,000 sat) is a member setting, with no hidden default the member cannot see.

### 4.5 No fee reserve

Gazpacho's owner decided there is no on-chain reserve in 1.0.0 (`docs/ux/decisions.md` decision 6). We are not asking you to reserve fees from members' leaves or to sign several fee tiers in advance.

There is one residual case: **a member with no on-chain coins cannot bump.** At rate 0 it cannot exit at all, and at 0.1 sat/vB it gets only what an anchor-only child can buy (your own note: about 1.7 sat/vB above the pre-signed rate). Gazpacho's wallet will say so plainly. Please keep the anchor-only child path and the message, and expose a clear "cannot complete this exit without an on-chain coin" result. Do not let it look like a stall.

### 4.6 Bound the excessive-fee refusal by the member, not by the process

Today one process-wide `--max-tree-fee-rate` must "agree" between the LSP and every client (`include/superscalar/factory.h:771-773`), and the 25% bound counts the whole factory's funding (`src/factory.c:2176-2180`). We ask for three things:
- the ceiling is a per-factory value the client compares with **its own** limit, and is not required to match the LSP's;
- the client gets a check on **its own path**: the sum of the pre-signed fees on the nodes from the root to its leaf, compared with its channel. 25% of the whole funding can be most of a small member's channel;
- the client refuses a rate the host raised above the proposal. Today the floor raises it silently.

### 4.7 Commitments

The commitment fee is hard-coded at 1000 sat/kvB for a 154 vB model of a 167 vB transaction (`src/fee.c:83-86`, `src/lsp_channels.c:251-254`, `src/client.c:378-381`). It is not a term.
- On any Core from 29.1 the 154 sat already relays on its own (0.92 sat/vB, above 0.1), so the bug we reported on 11 November (`154 < 167`) was a pre-29.1 default. It is still a model error, and a hard-coded 1 sat/vB is still the wrong price.
- We ask you to move commitments to the **zero-fee shape of BOLTs #1228**: TRUC, no fee, a zero-value ephemeral P2A anchor (F11, F15). The party closing pays through the anchor. This removes the commitment fee from balance accounting entirely, which also removes the conservation gap described at `src/lsp_channels.c:240-250`.
- If that is too much for this pull request, make the commitment rate the same agreed term as the tree rate (default 100), with the vsize computed exactly, as #476 already does for tree nodes.

### 4.8 Large payouts

Above about 230 members the payout becomes nVersion 2 (`src/factory.c:561-564`). A v2 parent with an anyone-can-spend anchor can be pinned. Before 31.0 it can be bumped in a package only if it meets the relay floor alone. With a minimal rate that is fragile.
- Please keep every payout under 10,000 vB so it stays TRUC. The end-date payout at every level (round 2, `2026-11-11-end-date-payout.md`) splits payouts by subtree, which does this naturally.
- Until then, refuse a factory whose payout would exceed 10,000 vB, rather than signing a v2 payout at a high fee.

### 4.9 Node version

TRUC and P2A need 28.0, ephemeral dust needs 29.0, and the 0.1 sat/vB default arrived in 29.1. Please make the check **29.1 or newer**, or at least warn when the node's `minrelaytxfee` is above the factory's pre-signed rate. Run CI on 29.1 and on the current release (32.1), not only 28.1.

## 5. The design we propose

1. **Terms.** The host's terms carry `tree_feerate` (Gazpacho term 32, sat/kvB, 0 to 1,000,000, default 100). The proposal carries it, and both sides build every node at exactly that rate for its exact vsize. No estimator enters the bytes.
2. **Anchors.** Every tree node, the payout and every commitment carries one P2A as its last output: zero value when the rate is 0 (ephemeral dust), 240 sat otherwise. The 16-output case must refuse rather than drop the anchor (our 11 November finding 4).
3. **TRUC everywhere,** with every transaction kept within 10,000 vB, and a tree child within 1,000 vB (F6). A level of 15 P2TR outputs plus an anchor is about 700 vB, so this holds.
4. **Exit, one level per block.** For each tree level on the member's path, starting at the root:
   - if the node pays the node's floor and the target, send it alone;
   - otherwise send it as a 1p1c package with a child on its anchor, funded from the member's wallet;
   - wait for it to confirm, then go to the next level.

   This matches F13 and what #476 already does. In the advanceless 1.0.0 shape every interior node is a fixed kickoff with nSequence `0xFFFFFFFE` and no relative lock (`src/factory.c:135-136`). So the only wait between levels is the TRUC one-ancestor rule, which costs one block per level, not a timelock. Each level's child can be funded from the change of the previous level's child once that confirms.
5. **Who pays.** As 4.4: the member for its own path, the host for its own transactions, payout receivers for the payout. If nobody gains, nobody pays and nothing confirms.
6. **Refusals at signing.** The member refuses a rate above its own ceiling, a fee on its own path above its own limit, any node paying other than the agreed rate, a payout over 10,000 vB, and a node without an anchor.
7. **Commitments.** Zero-fee (BOLTs #1228 shape). Failing that, the agreed rate with exact vsize. The 228-output cap stays.

## 6. What to test

Please run these with real daemons on regtest, and tell us the commit:

1. **Default relay on a current node.** Bitcoin Core 29.1 and 32.1, default settings (minrelay 0.1 sat/vB), tree rate 100 (0.1 sat/vB). Kill the LSP; each member force-closes alone. Every tree node relays alone, every commitment confirms, and the balances are exact. Repeat with members that have no wallet: they complete at 0.1 sat/vB using only the anchors.
2. **Rate 0.** The same drill with tree rate 0 and zero-value ephemeral anchors. Every level goes in as a package, and a member without a wallet gets a clear "needs an on-chain coin" result, not a stall.
3. **Fee spike.** Mempool minimum raised to 5, 20 and 100 sat/vB during the exit, after the factory was signed at 0.1 sat/vB. The member with a wallet completes, every level confirms one block after the previous, and the total paid is what the member's target asked for, within its budget. A member without a wallet gets the clear message.
4. **Patient exit.** The target is the cheapest rate that meets the path's deadline, not urgent. Show the fee paid against the urgent estimate.
5. **A node with 1 sat/vB relay** (pre-29.1 defaults, or `-minrelaytxfee=0.00001`). With TRUC packages the exit still completes.
6. **Griefing at the minimal rate.** The GRIEF=1 and GRIEF=wallet drills at tree rate 100 and at 0. Also a member that broadcasts a **sibling tree node** first to take the parent's single descendant slot; the exiting member's child must evict it.
7. **Host paths.** The host's sweeps and its rotation fallback pay from the host's wallet, and no member's coin is touched.
8. **Large factory.** 230 and 512 members: the payout is refused, or split so that each part is within 10,000 vB and TRUC.
9. **Refusals.** A client refuses: a rate above its own ceiling; a proposal whose nodes pay other than the stated rate; a host that raised the rate; a path whose fees exceed the member's limit; a node without an anchor.
10. **Commitments at 228 and 229 outputs** under the rate in use. 228 relays, and the 229th payment is refused with nothing changed.

## Two more findings from our fee research (reference main at 5f93110)

- `tools/superscalar_lsp.c:4018`: the host's own share is computed as `10000 − default × n` in a 16-bit field. We think this wraps when `default × n` exceeds 10,000 (our reading; not tested). Please check and refuse such settings.
- The default for `lsp_balance_pct` is 50 in the code and the operator guide but 100 in a header comment.
- More generally: the routing fee is an operator flag, not a signed term, and no client code checks it against a ceiling (`src/lsp_channels.c:790-796`). In Gazpacho the host's fees are signed terms with ranges that each member's software enforces; we would welcome the same in the reference.
