# Dedication

Gazpacho is dedicated to the people who paid for our freedom to hold our own keys.

## To Keonne Rodriguez and William Lonergan Hill

The developers of Samourai Wallet, a Bitcoin wallet that never held its users' coins. In November 2025 a United States court sentenced Keonne to five years in prison and Bill to four, after they pleaded guilty to conspiring to operate an unlicensed money transmitter. Developers across Bitcoin read the case as a threat to everyone who writes self-custody software. We build in the space they were punished for building in, and we do not forget it.

## To Roman Storm and Alexey Pertsev

Developers of Tornado Cash. In August 2025 a jury in New York convicted Roman of conspiring to operate an unlicensed money transmitting business, and could not agree on the more serious charges. In May 2024 a Dutch court sentenced Alexey to 64 months for money laundering over the same software; his appeal is pending. Both cases ask whether writing code that others use can be a crime.

## To those who came before

- **Phil Zimmermann**, who published PGP and spent three years under criminal investigation for it.
- **Daniel J. Bernstein**, whose case helped establish that code is speech.
- **The cypherpunks**, who wrote code instead of asking permission.
- **Hal Finney**, who received the first Bitcoin transaction.
- **Satoshi Nakamoto**, who gave the world a way to hold value without asking anyone.
- **ZmnSCPxj**, whose SuperScalar design this wallet is built on, and every Bitcoin and Lightning developer who works in the open.

## What we owe them

To make holding your own keys ordinary. To build software that cannot freeze, seize or spend what its users own, because it never holds it. To verify instead of trust. To write it in the open, under a free licence, so that it can be read, copied and carried on by anyone. To make freedom of money harder to take away, one more running copy at a time.

Every Gazpacho factory is one host and many members, each holding their own keys, none able to spend for another. That is the whole point.

*The facts above are as reported up to 2026. Sources: United States Attorney's Office, Southern District of New York (Samourai sentencing, November 2025); reporting by The Block, Decrypt, Blockworks and Bitcoin Magazine; DeFi Education Fund (Storm case update, March 2026).*
